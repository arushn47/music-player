# Lumaire v1.0.5

This update is about making Lumaire feel better to use every day: bring your playlists along, keep the music going, and follow the lyrics while doing something else.

## Your music, a little closer

- Import a playlist by pasting its shared link from Spotify, YouTube Music or YouTube. Importing the same playlist again no longer leaves you with another identical copy.
- Turn on Autoplay to keep listening after the queue ends. Next works with Autoplay too, and alternate versions of the same song are filtered out so you don't hear it twice in a row.
- Pressing Next pauses the current song immediately and shows loading on Play. Autoplay checks two audio sources at a time to reduce waits.
- Album downloads now show progress, with pause and cancel controls. Downloads keep going when you move between pages, and small offline badges make downloaded music easier to spot.

## Lyrics that fit the way you listen

- A desktop expanded player with large artwork, lyrics and a soft background drawn from the cover's colors, in both light and dark mode.
- A movable, resizable Windows lyrics overlay that stays above other apps. Adjust its background opacity, use playback controls, or lock it for a lyrics-only view. Hover the corner to unlock, or use Ctrl+Alt+L when the shortcut is available.
- Better matching for synced lyrics, including differences in punctuation, album names and recording length. Section labels no longer produce timestamp-warning clutter.
- Improved language handling, restored lyric display settings and safeguards against spending Gemini credits translating English-only lyrics into English.

## A tidier library

- Playlist covers now appear in the sidebar. The collapsed sidebar keeps scrolling clean with subtle fades and arrows when more playlists are out of view.
- Simpler playlist pages, clearer artist links, refined controls and more room for playlist names.
- Connect devices on the same Wi-Fi to share playlists and listen together without creating a Lumaire account.
- A new Support & contribute section with feedback, GitHub and social links.

## Updating from 1.0.4

The published **1.0.4 preview does not contain the in-app updater**. Download and run the **1.0.5 Windows installer once**, without uninstalling Lumaire first. Your existing library and settings stay in place. This release includes the updater for future signed Windows releases, with release notes before installation and a What's new view afterwards. Available updates appear beside Import in your accent color; regular update controls live in Settings → About.

On Android, install the newer APK over the existing app. Android will ask for confirmation; keep the existing app installed to retain its data. APK upgrades require the same signing certificate.

Audio availability still depends on the source. Device sync/listening together is for the same Wi-Fi, and the floating lyrics overlay and expanded player are desktop features. Optional AI lyric processing still requires your own key.
